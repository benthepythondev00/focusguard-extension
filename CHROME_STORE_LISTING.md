FocusGuard - Your Personal Focus & Productivity Timer

Stop losing hours to distractions. FocusGuard uses the proven Pomodoro Technique to help you work in focused bursts with strategic breaks. Perfect for developers, students, writers, and anyone who wants to get more done.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Click the FocusGuard icon to open the timer
2. Press Start to begin a 25-minute focus session
3. Work without distractions until the timer ends
4. Take a 5-minute break (or 15 minutes after 4 sessions)
5. Repeat and watch your productivity soar

SMART TIMER FEATURES
• Clean, minimalist circular timer display
• Visual progress ring shows session progress
• Badge counter shows remaining minutes
• Desktop notifications when sessions complete
• Pause and resume anytime

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• See today's completed focus sessions
• View total focus time
• Track breaks taken
• Weekly statistics with visual charts
• Session dots show progress toward long break

FULLY CUSTOMIZABLE
• Focus duration: 1-120 minutes (default 25)
• Short break: 1-30 minutes (default 5)
• Long break: 1-60 minutes (default 15)
• Sessions before long break: 1-10 (default 4)
• Auto-start breaks or focus sessions
• Sound and notification controls

BEAUTIFUL DESIGN
• Clean, modern interface
• Light, dark, and system themes
• Red accent for focus, green for breaks
• Responsive and smooth animations
• Professional minimalist aesthetic

PRIVACY FIRST

PERFECT FOR

THE POMODORO TECHNIQUE
Developed by Francesco Cirillo, this time management method uses a timer to break work into intervals (traditionally 25 minutes) separated by short breaks. This technique:
• Reduces mental fatigue
• Increases focus and concentration
• Creates sustainable work habits
• Helps track time spent on tasks
• Combats procrastination
