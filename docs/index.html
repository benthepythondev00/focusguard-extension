This policy covers FocusGuard - Pomodoro Timer & Break Reminder on the Chrome Web Store (id flabphbijkcfmdikjjnjhmgepfhmllhn).

Overview

FocusGuard ("the Extension") is a Chrome browser extension that helps you stay productive with Pomodoro-style focus sessions and break reminders. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

Key Point: All your data is stored locally on your device. We never collect, transmit, or have access to your timer data, statistics, or any personal information.

Data Collection and Storage

What Data We Store

The Extension stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Timer settings (focus duration, break duration, etc.)
  • Session statistics (number of completed sessions, total focus time)
  • Theme preferences (light/dark/system)
  • Notification preferences

How Data is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API (chrome.storage.local). Your data:

  • Never leaves your browser
  • Is not sent to any external servers
  • Is not shared with any third parties
  • Is not accessible to us or anyone else

Data You Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • View your statistics in the Extension popup
  • Adjust all settings at any time
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data

Permissions We Request

The Extension requests the following permissions:

  1. storage: To save your timer settings and statistics locally
  2. alarms: To run the timer countdown accurately in the background
  3. notifications: To alert you when focus sessions or breaks are complete

Data Sharing

We do NOT:

  • Collect any analytics or usage data
  • Send any data to external servers
  • Share your data with third parties
  • Track your browsing activity
  • Access any websites you visit

Third-Party Services

The free version of FocusGuard does not use any third-party services.

FocusGuard Pro is sold through Stripe Checkout (stripe.com). The extension keeps a random install key and asks our license check (income.146-59-68-85.nip.io) only whether that key, or the email you enter to restore a purchase, has an active Pro purchase. The check stores a one-way hash of the key and the checkout email, never your timer stats. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data: stripe.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

FocusGuard is suitable for users of all ages. We do not collect any personal data from any users, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device and we have no access to it, you can exercise all data rights directly through the Extension's interface or by uninstalling the extension.
